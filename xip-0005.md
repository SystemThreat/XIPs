## Abstract

The xCoin currency's ticker is **XID**, its supply cap is exactly
**100,000,000 XID** (10¹⁶ sat), and new coins are issued on one schedule, the
**Annual Tenth**: a short ramp, a flat 50 XID per block, then a step down of
10% roughly every year, never below 1.5 XID, fading to a 0.1 XID tail that
ends at block 35,375,353. Block 0 mints nothing: there is no premine and no
founder allocation. This replaces the 21,000,000 XCF cap and halving-era table
that XIP-3 froze in draft. It is a genesis rule of mainnet and was deployed
with block 0 on 2026-09-26.

## Motivation

The 21M, halving-every-750,000-blocks draft copied Bitcoin's shape: most coins
early, then cliffs every few years. Two things argued against it. A halving
cliff halves miner revenue overnight, which is the worst moment for a young
chain's security. And an early-heavy schedule gives the first year's miners a
share of all coins that later participants can never match. The Annual Tenth
spends its steepest issuance on a flat, predictable stretch, then declines in
small, frequent steps, so no year's miners are paid twice what the next
year's are.

The founder chose a cap of 100,000,000 over 21,000,000 on 2026-09-25, and
renamed the ticker from XCF to XID the same day; XID matches the `xid1…`
forum identities that already exist.

## Specification

All values are consensus rules on mainnet (`src/consensus/params.h`,
`src/validation.cpp` `GetBlockSubsidy`, public tree
[SystemThreat/xCoin](https://github.com/SystemThreat/xCoin), tag
`mainnet-genesis-2026-09-26`).

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Unit | 1 XID = 10⁸ sat |
| Cap | `MAX_SUPPLY_SAT = MAX_MONEY = 10,000,000,000,000,000` sat (100,000,000 XID) |
| Block 0 | mints nothing |
| Blocks 1–20,000 | 6.25 XID |
| Blocks 20,001–40,000 | 12.5 XID |
| Blocks 40,001–60,000 | 25 XID |
| Blocks 60,001–220,000 | 50 XID |
| From block 220,001 | 10% less every 110,000 blocks (45, 40.5, 36.45, …) |
| Floor | 1.5 XID, blocks 3,850,001–31,460,000 |
| Fade and tail | steps that never pay more than a tenth of what is left, then 0.1 XID to block 35,375,353 |
| Closing remainder | 1,660,000 sat, minted once in block 35,375,353 so the total is exactly the cap |
| After block 35,375,353 | subsidy zero: fees only |

The schedule is a 65-row table, `EMISSION_TABLE`, generated from
`contrib/regenesis/emission-policy.json` and never hand-edited. The node
refuses to compile unless the rows are contiguous from block 1 and
`sum(rows) + closing remainder == MAX_SUPPLY_SAT` exactly (static asserts).
SHA-256 of the table as the charter states it:
`356de0dce26aa5e5b9481c3936ecdefb1f81d14dd5e2ca2a43cc7b2775d0a230`.
The table is part of the charter (section 4), and the charter's hash is
committed in the genesis coinbase (XIP-6), so changing the schedule means a
different currency, not an upgrade.

At 300-second blocks from genesis, the schedule issues 25% of the cap around
2032, 50% around 2046, 75% around 2177 and 99% around 2330; the flat 50 XID
ends in late 2028, the 1.5 XID floor starts around 2063, and the last subsidy
block falls around 2363. Real dates drift with hashrate; the block heights do not.

## Rationale

- **The ramp (6.25 → 12.5 → 25 → 50).** The first 60,000 blocks are when the
  fewest people know the chain exists. A ramp keeps the earliest weeks from
  paying the full rate to whoever happened to be first.
- **10% steps instead of halvings.** Revenue falls a little each year rather
  than by half every four; miners and pools can plan around it.
- **The floor and the tenth-of-what-is-left rule.** A floor keeps a meaningful
  subsidy for centuries rather than letting it vanish into dust; capping each
  step at a tenth of the remainder guarantees the table can never overshoot the
  cap.
- **Rejected:** the 21M halving table (cliffs, front-loaded); a perpetual tail
  with no cap (breaks the fixed-supply promise); a founder allocation or lock
  (none: every coin is mined).
- **How it was chosen.** Candidate schedules were generated by an
  evolutionary search and reviewed by independent judge and red-team passes;
  the founder picked the winner, candidate g4-D3, on 2026-09-25.

## Backwards compatibility

A genesis rule of a new chain: nothing to be compatible with. Testnet A, which
ran the old 21M XCF rules, is retired and its ledger archived at height 3,555;
its coins have no value and do not carry over.

## Activation (Consensus only)

Chain: mainnet. Mechanism: genesis rule, active from block 1 (block 0 mints
nothing). Rehearsal: the table and its invariants are covered by the unit and
functional tests of the genesis release, and the shape ran on a short
rehearsal chain before genesis. Deployed 2026-09-26 with block 0 (XIP-6).

## Security considerations

A falling subsidy means the security budget must be taken over by fees in the
long run; the 1.5 XID floor delays that for roughly 260 years but does not
remove it. The exactness of the cap depends on the table and the closing
remainder, both enforced at compile time; a node built from a modified table
would be on a different currency and would not accept mainnet's genesis.
